# waste-biomethane-ccs-siting

Code and figure source data for the manuscript **“Waste biomethane–CCS enables cost-effective carbon removal in China”** (submitted to *Nature Sustainability*).

**Authors:** Leichang Cao, Chenlin Wei, Jieni Wang, Haodong Hou, Huilan Hou, Gang Luo, Angelidaki Irini, Daniel C.W. Tsang4, Yifei Sun, Shicheng Zhang, James H. Clark 


## Abstract
Meeting the Paris Agreement goals requires scalable carbon dioxide removal (CDR), yet conventional bioenergy with carbon capture and storage (BECCS) is constrained by land and siting. We map crop residues, livestock manure and food waste across China and optimise waste biomethane–CCS deployment using spatial siting, life–cycle assessment and techno–economic modelling. China’s waste biomass could supply 99.5 billion m3 yr-1 of biomethane. A rollout of 500 plants using 44% of this resource achieves total mitigation (E_Mit; net life–cycle balance plus avoided emissions from biomethane and digestate substitution) of 68.90 MtCO2eq yr-1 (95% CI 61.11-83.49), corresponding to gross geological CO2 storage (E_CDR) of 51.47 MtCO2 yr-1, at an average cost of -5.74 US$ tCO2eq-1. Cost–effective opportunities cluster in eastern Sichuan, Northwest China and parts of East and Central South China. A targeted incentive of 30 US$ tCO2eq-1 unlocks 65.63 MtCO2eq yr-1 of E_Mit.

## Repository contents
- `scripts/` — ArcGIS Pro (Python window) siting script (sliding-window hotspot selection with iterative feedstock deduction).
- `data/` — Figure source data and key input/summary tables (CSV).
