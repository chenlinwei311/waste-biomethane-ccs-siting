# waste-biomethane-ccs-siting

Code and figure source data for the manuscript **“Waste biomethane–CCS enables cost-effective mitigation and geological CO<sub>2</sub> storage in China”**.

**Authors:** Leichang Cao, Chenlin Wei, Jieni Wang, Haodong Hou, Huilan Hou, Gang Luo, Angelidaki Irini, Daniel C.W. Tsang, Yifei Sun, Shicheng Zhang, James H. Clark 


## Abstract
Scalable carbon dioxide removal is needed to meet climate goals, yet the deployable potential of waste-derived bioenergy with carbon capture and storage (BECCS) remains poorly constrained by sustainability and siting limitations. Here we map crop residues, livestock manure and food waste across China and integrate spatial siting, process-based life-cycle assessment and plant-level techno-economic analysis under uncertainty to evaluate waste biomethane–CCS. China's waste biomass has a theoretical biomethane potential of 99.5 billion m<sup>3</sup> yr<sup>−1</sup>. An optimized portfolio of 500 plants utilizing 44% of this potential delivers 68.90 MtCO<sub>2</sub>eq yr<sup>−1</sup> of total mitigation (E<sub>Mit</sub>, including substitution benefits from biomethane and digestate) at an average mitigation (abatement) cost of −US$5.74 tCO<sub>2</sub>eq<sup>−1</sup>, while storing 51.47 MtCO<sub>2</sub> yr<sup>−1</sup> as gross geological CO<sub>2</sub> (E<sub>CDR</sub>). Cost-effective opportunities cluster in eastern Sichuan, Northwest China, and parts of East and Central South China. Together, our results provide a transferable, sustainability-informed workflow for prioritizing waste-based biomethane–CCS deployment and for transparently separating durable geological storage from total mitigation.

## Repository contents
- `scripts/` — ArcGIS Pro (Python window) siting script (sliding-window hotspot selection with iterative feedstock deduction).
- `data/` — Figure source data and key input/summary tables (CSV).
